# Overview

The office home page — “what is happening now”: what is urgent in orders, what waits in the shop, what leaves today, how
many recuts. Owner, 9 Oct 2026: “I wouldn't flash money around — people don't need to see it” — Overview shows no
money. Figures for weeks and months are in Reports ([reports.en.md](reports.en.md)).

## Blocks

Every figure is clickable and opens its list with the matching view.

- **Orders** — **Due today**, **Due this week**, **Needs attention** (on Hold and overdue) open Sales with the same quick
  view; **To verify** and **To batch** — the Optimization queue.
- **Shop now** — two bars per station: **Waiting now** (for To batch — in the queue, for CUT — in batches) and **Done
  today**. Clicking a station opens Production → In production with only that station. A day is 0:00–24:00 by the
  computer's clock.
- **Shipping** — **Ready** (orders to ship), **Deliveries today**, **Open packing slips** (planned · shipped),
  **Backorders** — the same numbers as the Shipping tabs; below, **Today** lists today's packing slips.
- **Recuts · 7 days** — Recut glass of the last 7 days by the station where it broke.

## Who sees what

A block shows only if the person has its section ticked in Users: Orders — Sales or Optimization, Shop now and Recuts —
Production, Shipping — Shipping. None of them — “Nothing to show here.” A person whose sections were all removed lands on
an empty Overview.

## Own screens

The built-in Overview is the first tab, **Now** (tabs show once at least one report is placed for the person). Reports the administrator placed for a person in Reports (Edit → **Show on**,
needs the Screens box in Users) appear as tabs next to it: “Me” in a report filter is the signed-in person. A report is
counted only while its tab is open. Money sources stay closed here too without the Finance box.

At the bottom — the program version (build) and where the data lives.
